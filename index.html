Why Meridian

What makes this different

⊕
Zero infrastructure
No server. No licence. No data engineering team. Every byte of computation runs in your browser. Your data never leaves your machine — not as a marketing claim, as an architectural fact.
⬟
The framework is the output
Each module is a pre-built analytical framework — not a generic chart tool. The pocket price waterfall is actually a pocket price waterfall. The output is board-ready before you open the export dialog.
▦
Consulting IP as open infrastructure
The analytical frameworks embedded in each module are drawn from real commercial engagements — pricing transformation, consumer analytics, commercial diligence. Transparent, forkable, free.
