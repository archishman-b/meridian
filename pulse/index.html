P4 — Churn Prediction
Which customers are about to leave?
Scores every customer on churn risk using a logistic regression model trained on recency, frequency, monetary value, and trend signals. Segments customers into High / Medium / Low risk tiers with actionable retention recommendations.
⚠
Load data to run churn prediction
Churn model requires customer ID, transaction date, and transaction value. The model trains and scores entirely in your browser.
Model approach: Logistic regression on RFM signals — recency percentile, frequency percentile, monetary percentile, and recency trend (improving or declining). Trained on the loaded dataset using gradient descent. No server, no external model — your data, your browser.
